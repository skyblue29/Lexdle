    How scoring works

    • LEP = letter rarity points + pattern points + word bonus points.
    • Letter rarity: each letter earns 4 x -log2(its English frequency) points; repeated copies of a letter earn half.
    • Patterns: points come from each pattern's simulated probability. Within one overlap group only the best match counts, and surviving groups are weighted 1, 0.85, 0.85^2, ... from highest to lowest.
    • Words: bonus = length base (3:3, 4:8, 5:20, 6:50, 7:140) x rarity multiplier (Common 1 to Mythical 12). A 7-letter word is then multiplied by 1.5 and gains +100. Words are sorted best first and weighted 1, 0.85, 0.85^2, ...
    • Word rarity comes from real word frequency rank (OpenSubtitles); valid dictionary words missing from the frequency list are Mythical.
    • Overall tier and probability come from a simulation of millions of random rolls under this same formula.
